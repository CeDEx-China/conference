About the Conference

The 5th CeDEx China & 4th CHE Conference brings together scholars, policymakers, and healthcare leaders to explore decision-making behaviour, health system optimization, and economic approaches to public health challenges.

Organised by Centre for Decision Research and Experimental Economics China (CeDEx China) and the Centre for Health Economics (CHE), University of Nottingham Ningbo China.

Conference Topics

  • Experimental Economics & Game Theory
  • Behavioural Economics
  • Healthcare Policy Innovation
  • Global Health Systems
  • Behavioral Interventions
  • Health Services Research
  • Evidence-Based Strategies for Population Health
