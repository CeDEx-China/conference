About the Conference

The CeDEx China Annual Conference brings together researchers and practitioners in behavioral and experimental economics for two days of presentations, networking, and insightful discussions.

This year, we focus on bridging theoretical advances and practical applications, featuring special sessions on decision-making under uncertainty, experimental methods, and policy implications.

Conference Highlights

  • Keynote speeches from world-renowned economists
  • Parallel sessions covering diverse topics
  • Poster session for emerging researchers
  • Panel discussions on future directions
  • Networking reception and social events
  • Lab tours of CeDEx China facilities

Paper Submission

Call for Papers

We invite submissions of original research papers in all areas of behavioral and experimental economics, including but not limited to:

  • Decision-making under risk and uncertainty
  • Social preferences and other-regarding behavior
  • Experimental methods and design
  • Behavioral game theory
  • Neuroeconomics and psychology of economics
  • Field experiments and natural experiments
  • Behavioral public policy and nudges
  • Market design and mechanism design

Submission Guidelines

  • Papers should be submitted in PDF format
  • Maximum length: 12,000 words including references
  • Use standard academic formatting (12 pt font, double-spaced)
  • Include abstract (max 250 words) and JEL codes
  • Blind review process — remove author information
  • Submit via email to: CedexChina@nottingham.edu.cn
